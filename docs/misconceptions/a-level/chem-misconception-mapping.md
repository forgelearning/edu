# A-Level Chemistry misconception mapping

Measured baseline: 89 source questions, 89 one-question `MC-<id>` tags. The
retag groups questions by the error a learner is likely to make, across the
specification sections and organic/inorganic banks. Coverage twins inherit the
source tag.

| Tag | Error grouped |
|---|---|
| `MC-GCHEM-ATOMIC-STRUCTURE` | Atomic number, isotopes, electron shells and periodic trends are conflated |
| `MC-GCHEM-MOLES-FORMULAE` | Moles, relative mass and formula ratios are confused |
| `MC-GCHEM-BONDING-STRUCTURE` | Bond type, electron-pair origin and structure are mixed up |
| `MC-GCHEM-MASS-SPECTRA` | Mass spectra and analytical signals are interpreted as the wrong measurement |
| `MC-GCHEM-ENERGETICS` | Enthalpy, entropy, feasibility and energy transfer are interchanged |
| `MC-GCHEM-RATES` | Collision frequency, activation energy and rate equations are confused |
| `MC-GCHEM-EQUILIBRIA` | Equilibrium position, reaction quotient and rate are conflated |
| `MC-GCHEM-ACIDS-REDOX` | Acid-base, oxidation-state and electrode ideas are swapped |
| `MC-GCHEM-INORGANIC-TESTS` | Group trends, displacement and ion tests are treated as the same evidence |
| `MC-GCHEM-ORGANIC-MECHANISMS` | Organic reaction type is inferred from reagent name alone |
| `MC-GCHEM-FUNCTIONAL-GROUPS` | Functional groups and transformations between them are confused |
| `MC-GCHEM-ORGANIC-STRUCTURE` | Connectivity, stereochemistry, polymers and biomolecules are conflated |
| `MC-GCHEM-SYNTHESIS` | Synthesis routes omit the target, conditions or evaluation criteria |

All 13 tags have labels and commit-before-reveal starters in the teacher
heatmap data. Result: 13 tags, no source singletons, 0% mechanical tags.
