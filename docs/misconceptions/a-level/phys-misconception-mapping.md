# A-Level Physics misconception mapping

Measured baseline: 69 source questions, 69 one-question `MC-<id>` tags. The
retag groups the physical relationship or measurement error being made, not
the specification chapter. Coverage twins retain their source's tag.

| Tag | Error grouped |
|---|---|
| `MC-GPHYS-RESULTANT-FORCE` | Zero resultant force is confused with no forces |
| `MC-GPHYS-MOTION-GRAVITY` | Velocity, acceleration and gravitational mass effects are confused |
| `MC-GPHYS-WORK-ENERGY` | Work, energy, momentum and elastic energy relationships are swapped |
| `MC-GPHYS-SCALAR-VECTOR` | Magnitude-only and direction-bearing quantities are confused |
| `MC-GPHYS-GRAPHS-KINEMATICS` | Gradients and time constants are assigned to the wrong quantity |
| `MC-GPHYS-SPRINGS-MATERIALS` | Stiffness, extension, stress, strain and stored energy are conflated |
| `MC-GPHYS-CIRCUITS` | Current, resistance, potential difference and component roles are mixed up |
| `MC-GPHYS-WAVES` | Particle motion, wave travel, interference and intensity are confused |
| `MC-GPHYS-QUANTUM-NUCLEAR` | Quantum, particle, nuclear and photon evidence are interchanged |
| `MC-GPHYS-FIELDS-ELECTROMAGNETISM` | Different field effects and electromagnetic relationships are conflated |
| `MC-GPHYS-OSCILLATIONS-THERMAL` | SHM acceleration and internal energy are treated as the same model |
| `MC-GPHYS-ASTRONOMY` | Parallax, luminosity, red shift and imaging measure different quantities |
| `MC-GPHYS-MEASUREMENT` | Accuracy, precision, resolution, uncertainty and calibration are confused |

All 13 tags have labels and commit-before-reveal starters. Result: 13 tags,
no source singletons, 0% mechanical tags.
