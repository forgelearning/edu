# A-Level Biology misconception mapping

Measured baseline: 87 source questions, 87 one-question `MC-<id>` tags. The
retag follows the biological process being confused rather than the bank or
topic heading. Coverage twins retain their source's tag.

| Tag | Error grouped |
|---|---|
| `MC-GBIO-CELL-STRUCTURE` | Cell structures and their functions are assigned to the wrong level |
| `MC-GBIO-RESPIRATION-PHOTOSYNTHESIS` | Respiration and photosynthesis are treated as one energy pathway |
| `MC-GBIO-MEMBRANE-TRANSPORT` | Diffusion, osmosis, active transport and magnification are confused |
| `MC-GBIO-DNA-PROTEIN` | Replication, transcription, translation and peptide bonding are mixed up |
| `MC-GBIO-CELL-DIVISION` | Mitosis, meiosis and variation processes are conflated |
| `MC-GBIO-GENETICS` | Genotype, phenotype, inheritance and population allele frequency are swapped |
| `MC-GBIO-EVOLUTION` | Selection, classification, biodiversity and disease are treated as the same idea |
| `MC-GBIO-ENZYMES` | Enzyme structure, active sites, inhibitors and conditions are confused |
| `MC-GBIO-INVESTIGATION` | Reliability, uncertainty and control variables are used interchangeably |
| `MC-GBIO-CIRCULATION` | Pressure, vessels, haemoglobin and tissue fluid roles are mixed up |
| `MC-GBIO-HOMEOSTASIS` | Feedback components and specific kidney/neural mechanisms are conflated |
| `MC-GBIO-GENETIC-ENGINEERING` | Cloning, restriction enzymes and transgenic organisms are confused |
| `MC-GBIO-ECOLOGY-DISEASE` | Pathogens, decomposers, energy loss and carrying capacity are conflated |

All 13 tags have labels and commit-before-reveal starters. Result: 13 tags,
no source singletons, 0% mechanical tags.
