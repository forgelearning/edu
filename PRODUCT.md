# Forge Learning

Forge is misconception-triggered practice for GCSE and A Level students. A wrong multiple-choice answer maps to a specific mistaken idea, receives a concise corrective scaffold, and is followed by a fresh question that tests whether the idea was repaired.

## Primary users

- Students use the live quiz for private, targeted daily practice.
- Teachers use misconception patterns to choose the next useful corrective activity.
- School leaders can request updates when a new pilot route opens.

## Product truth

- The public quiz is available without an account.
- Questions are mapped to specification points and informed by examiner reports.
- Student and teacher product views shown on the landing page use clearly labelled illustrative data.
- Public-site analytics do not analyse student quiz responses.
- Subject pages are the source of truth for current banks and question totals.

## Landing-page conversion hierarchy

1. Try the live quiz.
2. Inspect how the misconception-repair loop works.
3. Review the student and teacher product views.
4. Inspect subject coverage and evidence.
5. Teachers and school leaders may request pilot updates.

## Voice

Direct, calm, precise, and teacher-led. Prefer concrete learning actions over generic edtech claims. Avoid inflated outcomes, surveillance language, and unsupported commercial proof.
