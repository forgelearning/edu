# Business and Computer Science misconception mapping

Business was measured at 112 source and 202 expanded questions; Computer
Science at 112 source and 212 expanded questions. Both were entirely
one-question-tagged at baseline. The taxonomies group the decision or system
error rather than the bank code.

## Business

`MC-GBUS-MARKET-RESEARCH`, `MC-GBUS-PRICING`,
`MC-GBUS-OWNERSHIP-STAKEHOLDERS`, `MC-GBUS-PROFIT-BREAKEVEN`,
`MC-GBUS-CASH-FINANCE`, `MC-GBUS-PRODUCT-GROWTH`, `MC-GBUS-OPERATIONS`,
`MC-GBUS-STRATEGY-DECISIONS`, `MC-GBUS-INTEREST-CURRENCY`,
`MC-GBUS-GLOBAL-TRADE`, and `MC-GBUS-ETHICS-RISK` separate marketing,
finance, operations, strategy and international-business errors. Result: 11
shared tags, no singletons, 0% mechanical tags.

## Computer Science

`MC-GCS-PROGRAMMING-ERRORS`, `MC-GCS-ABSTRACTION`, `MC-GCS-ALGORITHMS`,
`MC-GCS-DATA-STRUCTURES`, `MC-GCS-OOP`, `MC-GCS-NETWORK-PROTOCOLS`,
`MC-GCS-CRYPTOGRAPHY`, `MC-GCS-DATABASES`, `MC-GCS-SECURITY`,
`MC-GCS-ETHICS`, `MC-GCS-HARDWARE-ARCHITECTURE`,
`MC-GCS-DATA-REPRESENTATION`, and `MC-GCS-OPERATING-SYSTEMS` separate
programming, data, networks, security, architecture and ethics. Result: 13
shared tags, no singletons, 0% mechanical tags.

Every tag in both subjects has a human-readable label and a
commit-before-reveal starter with an answer.
