# Health & Social Care misconception mapping

Measured baseline: 62 source questions and 212 expanded questions, all on
one-question tags. The map keeps care values, professional procedures,
communication, anatomy and mental health distinct while reusing the same error
category across banks.

| Tag | Error grouped |
|---|---|
| `MC-GHSC-DEVELOPMENT` | Cognitive, physical, emotional and psychosocial development are different |
| `MC-GHSC-APPLICATION` | Assessment answers must apply care knowledge and weigh limitations |
| `MC-GHSC-PERSON-CENTRED` | Autonomy, empowerment, dignity and person-centred care protect choice |
| `MC-GHSC-EQUALITY` | Equality means fair access and adjustment, not identical treatment |
| `MC-GHSC-SAFEGUARDING` | Confidentiality, safeguarding, whistleblowing and duty of care use different procedures |
| `MC-GHSC-RISK-PRACTICE` | Hazards, risks, controls and multidisciplinary practice are different safety ideas |
| `MC-GHSC-RECORDS-CONSENT` | Records, boundaries and informed consent protect different aspects of care |
| `MC-GHSC-COMMUNICATION` | Empathy, listening, jargon and accessibility are different communication issues |
| `MC-GHSC-PHYSIOLOGY` | Organs and processes such as gas exchange and circulation have distinct roles |
| `MC-GHSC-PHYSICAL-HEALTH` | Fracture, arthritis, osteoporosis and movement are related but distinct |
| `MC-GHSC-MENTAL-HEALTH` | Wellbeing, depression, CBT and care planning are different levels |
| `MC-GHSC-PUBLIC-HEALTH` | Vectors and population prevention describe different disease interventions |

Every tag has a label and a commit-before-reveal starter. Result: 12 shared
tags, no singletons, 0% mechanical tags.
