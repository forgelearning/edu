Question 1 of 2
Finance · Cash flow vs profitConcept

A student writes: "If a business is making a profit, it can't go bankrupt — profit means it has enough money." Why is this wrong?

Exam answers on business failure need to name cash flow specifically, not just say "money problems" — this is one of AQA's most-tested finance distinctions ✦

Business in the real world · Limited liabilityApplication

A student writes: "Priya owns shares in a private limited company that goes bust owing £2 million. Because she's a shareholder, she's personally liable for that £2 million." Why is this wrong?

Application questions expect the specific term "limited liability" linked to the correct business type — not a vague "the company protects its owners" ✦
