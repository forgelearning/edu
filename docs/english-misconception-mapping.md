# English Literature and Language misconception mapping

The two subjects share the same 16 source questions and 200 expanded
questions, so they share one seven-tag taxonomy. The grouping follows the
exam-method error rather than the paper label.

| Tag | Error grouped |
|---|---|
| `MC-GENG-LEXICAL-SYNTAX` | Semantic fields, polysyndeton and lexical repetition need precise terms |
| `MC-GENG-VIEWPOINT-IRONY` | Narrative viewpoint and dramatic irony depend on different knowledge gaps |
| `MC-GENG-CONTEXT-REGISTER` | Deixis, register and context must be linked to meaning and audience |
| `MC-GENG-TONE-METAPHOR` | Bathos and metaphor require analysis of deliberate effects |
| `MC-GENG-AO2-METHOD` | Technique spotting is not analysis of how choices construct meaning |
| `MC-GENG-INTERPRETATION` | Exploration and personal response require alternative or evaluative readings |
| `MC-GENG-COMPARISON-FORM` | Comparison and form need meaningful structural connections |

Every tag has a label and a starter that requires a judgement before its
answer is revealed. Both `englit` and `engll` now measure 0% single-use and 0%
mechanical tags.
