# GCSE Geography — geographical skills misconception mapping

Eighth curated bank of the `gcse-geo` retag. The generated skills tags were
one per question, even when several questions tested the same calculation,
map convention or interpretation rule.

## Result

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| Distinct tags | 35 | 10 |
| Single-use tags | 35 | 0 |
| Questions per tag | 1.0 | 3.5 |

All 35 questions are mapped. Every tag has a label and a corrective starter
with an answer.

Subject-wide `gcse-geo` moves from 185 single-use questions (28.9%) to 150
(23.4%). The single-use ratchet moves from 0.30 to 0.24; the mechanical
ratchet remains 0.

## The organising idea: operation, not question format

The bank contains maps, charts, calculations, photographs and decision-making,
but the useful intervention is the operation a student has confused:

- four-figure and six-figure references are different levels of precision;
- a map scale converts map distance to ground distance, including curved routes
  and enlarged copies;
- contour spacing, V-shapes, spot heights and cross-sections describe relief in
  different ways;
- graph choice must match the data, and correlation is not causation;
- percentages, percentage points, index values, range, mode and density use
  different operations;
- description, sketching and evaluation require different evidence and command
  words.

## Grouping decisions worth knowing

- **Grid references are separate from general map conventions.** Questions 01,
  02 and 07 test easting/northing order and precision. Questions 05, 12 and 25
  test bearings, choropleth limitations and the legend.
- **Contour interpretation is separate from relief representation.** Questions
  06, 22 and 23 test what contour spacing, V-shapes and gradient mean. Questions
  21, 26 and 29 test spot heights, cross-sections and relief calculations.
- **Chart selection groups different visual forms under one error:** choosing a
  chart because it looks familiar rather than matching its data structure.
  Questions 09, 11, 33, 34 and 35 therefore share a starter that makes the
  data-to-representation rule explicit.
- **Percentage calculation keeps its denominator visible.** Questions 10, 13,
  14, 27 and 30 are grouped because students swap raw change, percentage
  change, percentage points, sector angle and index interpretation.
- **Prefix `MC-GG-SKI-` was checked across the three data files before use.**
