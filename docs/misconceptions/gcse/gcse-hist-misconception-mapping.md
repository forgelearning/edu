# GCSE History misconception mapping

The four GCSE History banks contain 200 questions. Before this pass every
question had a single-use bank-position tag; they now use 29 shared
`MC-GHIST-*` categories, with 0 single-use and 0 mechanical tags. Each tag has
an error-focused label and an explicit commit-before-reveal starter.

The organising principle is historical reasoning rather than period labels:
cause versus trigger, policy versus consequence, change versus continuity,
significance, and balanced judgement. The America, Interwar, Health and
Elizabeth banks remain distinct in the question set, while the tags describe
the misconception a teacher can intervene on.

| bank | organising groups | questions |
|---|---|---:|
| America | uneven prosperity; Depression and New Deal; civil rights; culture; war and equality | 54 |
| Interwar | settlement; road to war; dictatorship; appeasement; alliances; judgement; consequences | 54 |
| Health | beliefs; prevention; medicine; public health; industrialisation; government; judgement | 46 |
| Elizabeth | religion; royal power; challenges; foreign policy; Armada; society; judgement | 46 |

All 29 groups have explicit id-keyed assignments, labels and starters. The
starter asks the learner to compare, sequence, classify or evaluate before the
historical explanation is revealed.
