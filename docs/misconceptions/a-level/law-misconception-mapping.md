# A-Level Law misconception mapping

The four Law banks contain 200 questions. Before this pass every question had
a single-use bank-position tag; they now use 22 shared `MC-GLAW-*` categories,
with 0 single-use and 0 mechanical tags. Every category has an error-focused
label and an explicit commit-before-reveal starter.

The organising principle is the legal distinction students can swap: actus
reus versus mens rea, offence severity, court roles, duty versus breach,
causation, contract formation and remedies. The map keeps criminal law,
criminal procedure, tort and contract mechanisms separate where the corrective
rule differs, while grouping repeated definition questions inside each error.
