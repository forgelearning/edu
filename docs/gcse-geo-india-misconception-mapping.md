## The organising idea: mechanism, not case-study fact

The bank mixes India's global role, development, population, urbanisation and
environment. The useful groups are the errors students make when they confuse
scale, structure, mechanism or consequence:

- economic size is not the same as average income, and neither is an exact
  measure of inequality;
- services can dominate GDP while agriculture remains a major employer;
- education, skills, TNCs, FDI and special economic zones are related but not
  interchangeable mechanisms;
- national growth or aid does not mean equal development between regions;
- urbanisation, migration and demographic change have both opportunities and
  costs;
- pollution must be traced from source through air, water or waste pathways to
  health and productivity consequences.

## Grouping decisions worth knowing

- **Global influence is separate from investment policy.** Questions 01, 13,
  25, 39 and FB-08 test evidence of India's international weight. Questions 03,
  14 and 33 test why firms invest and how incentives trade off against public
  revenue.
- **FDI impact is separate from FDI attraction.** Questions 15, 19, 26 and 40
  ask students to balance flows, benefits, leakage and uneven outcomes; they
  are not just another definition of TNC or FDI.
- **Urbanisation is separate from population mobility.** Questions 06, 20, 23
  and FB-05 test the opportunity/pressure created by city growth. Questions 09,
  30 and 35 test how movement redistributes people, workers and investment.
- **GDP share is not employment share.** Question 02, 31 and FB-03 sit in
  economic structure; questions 16, 17, 32 and 34 sit in skills and higher-value
  development because their tested mechanism is education or IT capability.
- **FB-01 is grouped with site, situation and flow representation.** Its
  location facts are used as geographical position and scale evidence, rather
  than being treated as a separate India-facts tag.
- **Prefix `MC-GG-IND-` was checked across the three data files before use.**
