Privacy & Data

How Forge collects, uses and protects personal data — for students, teachers, and schools. Schools should review the proposed processing terms below with their data protection lead before agreeing to a pilot or licence.

Plain-English summary — Forge collects only what it needs to work. Students using the free tier enter only a name. Students joining a class provide a display name and class code; some classes also require an individual access code. No student email address is required. Students who create a Pro or trial account provide an email address and password — these are stored securely via Supabase Auth and never shared with third parties for commercial purposes. We do not sell data or run advertising profiles. Public Forge pages use Google Analytics 4 only after an analytics consent choice; student quiz data is not used for site analytics. Teachers use authenticated email/password accounts, with invite codes required for new teacher accounts.

1. Who we are

Forge is operated by Forge Education ("we", "us", "our"). We are the data controller for information we use for our own purposes, such as direct account, billing and public-site activity (forgelearning.github.io and any successor domain).

For a school-directed class pilot or licence, the school normally decides why pupils' learning data is used and is the data controller; Forge processes that class data on the school's instructions. Forge may be a separate controller for its own account, billing and public-site activity. The school and Forge should confirm these roles and the processing terms before use.

Our contact details are set out in Section 11.

2. What personal data we collect

Free-tier students (no account)

Students using the free tier do not create an account. They enter only:

  • A display name (first name or chosen alias — we do not require a surname)

A random anonymous identifier is generated and stored in their browser (localStorage) and in our database to persist progress on that device. No email address, password, or personal contact information is collected.

Students joining a class

Students joining a teacher's class enter a display name and class code. Classes with individual student access codes also require the private code issued by the teacher; older classes may allow joining with name and class code alone. A class association and a student identifier are stored so work can be linked to the right pupil. No student email address is required for class access.

A first name linked to a class and answer history can identify a pupil to their teacher. Class codes and individual access codes must be kept within the intended class. Where individual codes are enabled, the database stores a hash of the code rather than its plaintext value.

Class friends and the weekly league

Class friends. This feature is off by default. A teacher may enable it only for their class, and it stays paused until every student in that class has an individual private access code, so a name alone cannot be used to accept a request on someone else's behalf. When enabled, classmates can see names in a classmate picker and send requests. Only after a student accepts a request can the pair see each other's display name, weekly and total XP, answer count, accuracy and practice streak. Either student can remove the connection at any time. Teachers can see their class's connections and switch the feature off. There is no messaging between students.

Weekly class league. Students in a class can see a weekly XP league for that class. Classmates appear as first name and last initial, only students who have earned XP that week are listed, and each student sees the top three places plus the places either side of their own. The league resets every Monday, and teachers can switch it off for a class. A student who finishes first in a week where at least three classmates earned XP wins a trophy, and classmates in the league see how many trophies each listed student has won. Trophies are worked out from saved answers; no other detail of past weeks is shown.

Friends for students not in a class. This feature is switched off. If it is switched on, it will be available only to students who have signed in with an account, are not using Forge through a class, and confirm they are 13 or older. It is off for each student until they turn it on in Settings and choose the first name friends will see. There is no search and no list of other students: a student can only add someone who has given them their private friend code, and nothing about either student is shared until the other accepts. Accepted friends see each other's first name and initial, weekly and total XP, answer count, accuracy and practice streak. There is no messaging. A student can make a new code at any time, which stops the old one working, remove a friend, or turn the feature off, which deletes all of their friendships and requests.

Reporting a problem with a question. Any student can use “Report a problem with this question” after answering. We store which question it was, the reason chosen, the option the student picked, an optional note of up to 500 characters, and which student sent it, so a student can only send one report per question and at most 20 a day. Reports are read only by the Forge team, to correct questions; they are never shown to other students or to teachers. Please do not include personal details in the note. Reports are deleted when the student’s record is deleted. Legal basis: legitimate interests (keeping questions accurate).

Teachers (creating a class)

Teachers access the dashboard with an authenticated email address and password. New teacher accounts also require a school-provided invite code. We collect:

  • Email address and a password hash for authentication
  • Teacher account and class ownership data needed to create and manage classes
  • School-provided invite code during account creation; the code is validated and is not used as a student credential

For teachers on paid institutional licences, we may hold contact details (name, school email, school name) provided during the purchase process for billing and support purposes.

Waitlist sign-ups

If you submit your email address via the waiting list form, we collect your email address only. It is stored with Formspree (see Section 9) and used only to contact you about Forge pilots and launch. You can request removal at any time by emailing us.

Technical data

Hosting, database and other infrastructure providers may process IP addresses and browser information to deliver and secure the service. We are confirming each provider's logging and retention settings for the school pilot; these records are separate from pupil answer records.

4. Your rights under UK GDPR

If you are based in the UK or EEA, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restriction — ask us to limit how we process your data
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address in Section 11. We will respond within one calendar month.

Where a student's data is processed under a school DPA, rights requests relating to that student should be directed to the school in the first instance, as the data controller. The school may then request action from us as processor.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or by phone on 0303 123 1113.

6. Security

Application data is stored in Supabase (see Section 9), which provides:

  • Encryption in transit and at rest
  • Row-level security policies — students can only read their own responses
  • SOC 2 Type II certification
  • A project region that must be confirmed for each school pilot

Student Pro/trial accounts are authenticated using Supabase Auth. Passwords are stored as non-reversible hashes by the authentication provider. Session tokens are stored in browser localStorage for signed-in accounts.

Teacher dashboard access is controlled by authenticated teacher accounts. Class codes identify a class; where enabled, private student codes authenticate individual class access. Codes should be shared only with the intended pupils, and each individual code should go only to its assigned pupil. In older classes without individual codes, anyone with the class code and a pupil's display name may be able to rejoin that pupil's record; schools should enable individual codes for class pilots.

If we discover a personal data breach involving school-controlled data, we will notify the school without undue delay and provide information needed for its assessment. The school, as controller, decides whether notification to the ICO or affected people is required. Where Forge is controller, we assess and make any required notifications ourselves.

7. Cookies and local storage

Forge uses browser localStorage for essential preferences and sessions. Public-site analytics may use cookies after an analytics consent choice. Local storage is used to:

  • Remember your theme preference (dark/light mode)
  • Store your session (display name, class code and, for protected class-linked students, private student code) so you do not have to re-enter it when navigating between the Practice, Assignments, Repair mistakes, Timed practice, Home and Profile pages
  • Store your anonymous free-tier identifier (a randomly generated token, not linked to any personal information)
  • Store your Supabase Auth session token (access token + refresh token) if you have a Pro or trial account — this allows you to stay logged in across visits without re-entering your password
  • Remember appearance choices (accent colour, background and avatar character) and which library videos you have opened, so the Videos page can mark them as watched

localStorage data is stored on your device only and is never transmitted to our servers except as part of normal authenticated requests. It can be cleared at any time by clicking "Switch student" / "Sign out" or clearing your browser data.

Marketing and guide pages use Google Analytics 4 to understand site usage. Analytics storage is denied until a visitor gives consent through the site banner. Pupil learning pages do not load Google Analytics or the separate product event tracker. You can reject analytics or . Google may process technical request data under its own privacy terms; see the sub-processors section below.

The Videos library plays YouTube videos inside Forge using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). Nothing is loaded from YouTube until a student presses a video; from then on, YouTube receives technical request data such as the IP address, and processes it under Google's privacy policy, as it would on youtube.com. Forge does not send YouTube a student's name, class or answers. A few videos whose owners do not allow embedding open on youtube.com instead.

8. Proposed processing terms for schools

For school review

This agreement is entered into between the school or institution purchasing a Forge licence ("Controller") and Forge Education ("Processor"). It supplements and forms part of the Forge licence agreement. For a school pilot, these terms require review and agreement by an authorised school representative; a waitlist entry alone does not accept them.

8.1 Subject matter and duration

The Processor shall process personal data on behalf of the Controller for the purpose of providing the Forge educational platform, including Practice, Repair mistakes, Timed practice, and the Teacher Dashboard. Processing shall continue for the duration of the licence and for the retention periods set out in Section 5.

8.2 Nature of processing

Processing includes: collection, storage, retrieval, consultation, use, and disclosure by transmission of student question responses, misconception tags, display names, and XP data, as described in Section 2.

8.3 Categories of data subjects

Students enrolled in classes created by the Controller's teachers. Teaching and administrative staff of the Controller who access the Teacher Dashboard.

8.4 Types of personal data

Display names (not necessarily legal names), question response data, misconception tags, XP and streak data, friend connections between classmates, session timestamps. See Section 2 for the full list.

8.5 Processor obligations

The Processor shall:

  • Process personal data only on documented instructions from the Controller (which includes this agreement and any configuration made through the platform)
  • Ensure that persons authorised to process the data are subject to confidentiality obligations
  • Implement appropriate technical and organisational measures as described in Section 6
  • Respect the conditions for engaging sub-processors as set out in Section 9
  • Assist the Controller in responding to requests from data subjects exercising their rights under UK GDPR
  • Assist the Controller in ensuring compliance with its obligations regarding security, breach notification, data protection impact assessments, and prior consultation
  • Delete or return all personal data upon termination of the licence, at the Controller's choice, and delete existing copies unless storage is required by applicable law
  • Make available to the Controller all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this agreement

8.6 Sub-processing

The Controller grants general authorisation for the Processor to engage the sub-processors listed in Section 9. The Processor shall inform the Controller of any intended changes to sub-processors, giving the Controller the opportunity to object. Where the Controller objects and the parties cannot resolve the objection, the Controller may terminate the licence.

8.7 International transfers

The active Supabase project region and any transfer safeguards should be confirmed in the school's DPIA and processing agreement. Marketing and guide analytics may involve Google's global service after consent; pupil learning pages do not load Google Analytics. Any restricted transfer of school data requires an appropriate safeguard.

8.8 Controller obligations

The Controller shall:

  • Ensure it has a lawful basis for processing student data and has fulfilled any transparency obligations to students and parents under UK GDPR (e.g. by including Forge in its school privacy notice)
  • Ensure that class codes and, where enabled, private student codes are distributed only to enrolled students, with each private code given to its assigned pupil
  • Notify the Processor promptly if it becomes aware of any breach involving Forge data
  • Not instruct the Processor to process data in a way that would violate applicable data protection law

8.9 Liability and indemnity

Each party shall be responsible for compliance with its own obligations under UK GDPR. The Processor's liability to the Controller under this agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid by the Controller in the twelve months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

8.10 Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.

10. Deleting your Pro or trial account

If you have a Pro or trial account and wish to delete it:

  • Email us at forge.educator@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account, with the subject line "Delete my account"
  • We will delete your account and all associated personal data (email, password hash, subjects, responses) within 30 days and confirm by email
  • A pseudonymised transaction record (no email, no name) will be retained for 7 years if you held a paid subscription, for accounting compliance

Free-tier students (no account) can delete their local data by clearing their browser's localStorage or by clicking "Switch student" in the quiz. Contact us to request deletion of their server-side record; clearing the browser alone does not delete server-side data.

If you have also linked your account to a teacher's class, deletion of your account will remove you from that teacher's dashboard.
